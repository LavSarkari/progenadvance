Representative Research Experience

Structure-Based Drug Discovery • Antiviral Therapeutics

AI-Driven Discovery of Novel RdRp Inhibitors for Dengue Virus

Dengue virus remains a global health threat with limited antiviral options. We characterized the conserved RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) catalytic domain, screened diverse chemical libraries using RDKit similarity search, and evaluated binding energetics via 100ns molecular dynamics.

Key Scientific Finding

Interaction stability and binding free energies (MM/GBSA) proved substantially more reliable indicators of antiviral potency than docking scores alone.

AI/ML Predictive Modeling • Cardiovascular Target

Machine-Learning-Guided Identification of CETP Inhibitors

Cholesteryl Ester Transfer Protein (CETP) plays a central role in lipid metabolism. By combining pharmacophore modeling with a high-accuracy machine learning classifier (Score: 0.87), we evaluated sustained interactions within the CETP tunnel region.

Key Scientific Finding

Simulations mapped critical stabilizing residues within the hydrophobic tunnel, establishing a validated pipeline for structure-guided lead prioritization.
