Our Collaboration Philosophy

Science First: From Ideas to Impactful Outcomes

At ProGen Advance Insilico Solutions LLP, we believe scientific progress is driven by collaboration. We work closely with researchers and organizations to provide reliable, transparent, and customized computational solutions aligned with their scientific objectives.

We are committed to making advanced bioinformatics and computational biology accessible to students, academic researchers, startups, and established organizations. Flexible pricing and customized support options are structured depending on individual project scope and institutional grant requirements.

Core Collaboration Mantra

"From research ideas to scientific outcomes — We collaborate, compute, and create impact."

Flexible Engagement Models
Project-Based Milestone Engagements Clearly defined scopes of work (e.g., virtual screening campaign, RNA-Seq differential expression, or 100ns MD simulation) with agreed milestones and transparent timelines.
Retained In Silico Partnership Dedicated computational hours per month serving as an embedded bioinformatics team for biotechnology startups, CROs, and discovery groups.
Academic Thesis & Grant Support Subsidized academic rates assisting MSc and PhD scholars with publication-grade figures, methodologies, and statistical interpretations.
