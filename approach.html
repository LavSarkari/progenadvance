Methodology & Architecture

Our Scientific Research Approach

A unified, 5-stage computational framework transforming biological questions into actionable, publication-quality scientific discoveries.

01
Stage 01 • Initiation

Biological Question Formulation

Every project begins with a clear, biologically grounded hypothesis. We align with your research team to define the therapeutic target, disease pathways, clinical unmet needs, or genomic phenomena under investigation.

Disease Mechanisms
Oncology, viral pathogenesis, metabolic syndrome & rare genetic disorders.
Drug Discovery Scope
Target identification, allosteric pocket characterization & repurposing strategies.
Precision Medicine
Patient stratification hypotheses and neoantigen targeting rationales.
02
Stage 02 • Curation

Data Collection & Preparation

Robust computation demands clean, structured data. We curate, normalize, and format multi-source biological inputs, ensuring high fidelity before executing computational algorithms.

Structures & Sequences
PDB, AlphaFold predicted models, cryo-EM structures & UniProt FASTA sequences.
Omics Repositories
Raw NGS FASTQ reads, TCGA cohorts, GEO gene expression & PRIDE mass-spectrometry files.
Chemical Libraries
ChEMBL, PubChem, DrugBank, Zinc20 & proprietary client compound collections.
03
Stage 03 • In Silico Execution

Advanced Computational Analysis

Deploying state-of-the-art computational biophysics and machine learning algorithms on high-performance Linux computing environments to model biological interactions at molecular resolution.

AI & Machine Learning
Deep neural networks, pharmacophore screening & predictive bioactivity modeling.
Biophysical Simulation
100ns to 1μs explicit-solvent MD simulations, RMSD/RMSF & MM/GBSA energetics.
Bioinformatics Pipelines
Automated RNA-Seq alignment, variant calling, count normalization & differential expression.
04
Stage 04 • Biological Context

Scientific Interpretation

Data points and binding scores become impactful only when contextualized within systems biology. We translate algorithmic numbers into meaningful physiological mechanisms and pathway dynamics.

Biological Pathways
KEGG, Reactome & GSEA pathway enrichment identifying cascade modulation.
Interactions & Networks
Protein-ligand binding modes, per-residue energy decomposition & PPI network topology.
Mechanism Deconvolution
Understanding allosteric pocket transitions, tunnel entrapment & resistance mutations.
05
Stage 05 • Translation

Actionable Research Outcomes

Delivering complete, publication-ready assets and decision-support dossiers that enable your scientific team to progress candidates into wet-lab validation or clinical development.

Prioritized Lead Molecules
Filtered candidate cohorts with verified binding energetics and synthetic feasibility.
Validated Biomarkers
Robust multi-gene expression signatures correlated with clinical outcomes.
Publication Dossiers
Vector graphics, method sections, raw coordinate files & decision support reports.

Our Scientific Research Commitment

• Scientifically Rigorous Analysis: State-of-the-art force fields, strict convergence criteria, and statistical FDR corrections.
• Reproducible Computational Workflows: All parameters, scripts, and seeds documented for full reproducibility.
• Customized Solutions: Pipelines adapted to the exact biological constraints of your organism and target.
• Complete Confidentiality: Enforced through binding Non-Disclosure Agreements and private compute clusters.
• Experimental Boundary: Experimental wet-lab validation is performed by collaborating laboratories or client organizations where applicable. ProGen delivers high-quality analysis, scientific interpretation, and decision-support.
