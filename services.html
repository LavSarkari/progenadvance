Therapeutic Acceleration

AI-Driven Drug Discovery

Integrating artificial intelligence with computational biology to accelerate therapeutic discovery, repurpose existing drugs, and prioritize novel chemical scaffolds.

Traditional drug screening entails extensive timelines and high attrition rates. ProGen applies machine learning architectures and deep neural networks to vast chemical spaces, predicting bioactivity, identifying potential off-target effects, and accelerating lead generation.

Technical Capabilities:

  • ✔ Drug Repurposing & Lead Identification: Machine learning screening of approved drugs and clinical candidates for novel therapeutic indications.
  • ✔ AI/ML-Based Predictive Modeling: Quantitative Structure-Activity Relationship (QSAR) and graph neural networks for affinity prediction.
  • ✔ Chemical Library Prioritization: Screening millions of small-molecule compounds down to high-confidence candidate cohorts.
Deliverables & Technical Outputs
Prioritized Lead Scaffolds Ranked compound tables with predicted bioactivity scores, synthetic accessibility scores, and pharmacokinetic metrics.
Model Performance Telemetry Validation statistics including ROC-AUC curves, Precision-Recall benchmarks, and feature importance analyses.
Comprehensive Technical Dossier Publication-grade reports with detailed computational parameters, citations, and experimental recommendations.
Biophysical Modeling

Structure-Based Drug Design (SBDD)

From target structure resolution to lead optimization through advanced molecular modeling, docking, and all-atom molecular dynamics simulations.

We examine the thermodynamic and conformational realities of macromolecular complexes. By coupling rigid/flexible molecular docking with multi-nanosecond MD trajectories and MM/GBSA free energy calculations, we quantify binding affinity with high biophysical accuracy.

Technical Capabilities:

  • ✔ Protein Modeling & Engineering: Homology modeling, structural refinement, loop modeling, and binding pocket characterization.
  • ✔ Molecular Docking & HTVS: Target-ligand and protein-protein docking across diverse conformer ensembles.
  • ✔ Advanced Molecular Dynamics (MD): 100ns to 1μs explicit-solvent simulations to evaluate conformational stability and RMSD/RMSF.
  • ✔ Binding Free Energy Calculations: Molecular Mechanics Generalized Born Surface Area (MM/GBSA) and per-residue decomposition.
Structural Analysis Suite
Per-Residue Energy Decomposition Identification of key hydrogen bonds, hydrophobic contacts, and electrostatic anchors in the target binding pocket.
Conformational Stability Metrics Time-series trajectories of RMSD, radius of gyration, solvent-accessible surface area (SASA), and secondary structure persistence.
3D Structural Figures & Trajectories High-resolution ray-traced molecular renderings ready for publication in peer-reviewed scientific journals.
Genomics & Systems Biology

Multi-Omics Data Analysis

Transforming complex high-throughput omics data into meaningful biological insights through advanced bioinformatics and systems biology.

Next-generation sequencing and high-density mass spectrometry yield complex, multidimensional datasets. We deconvolute this biological complexity to reveal differentially regulated genes, enriched signaling cascades, and robust candidate biomarkers.

Technical Capabilities:

  • ✔ Genomics, Transcriptomics & Proteomics: End-to-end processing of raw sequencing reads (FASTQ) and quantitative proteomic profiles.
  • ✔ NGS Data Processing: Quality control, alignment to reference genomes (GRCh38), count normalization, and differential expression analysis (DESeq2/edgeR).
  • ✔ Functional Annotation & Pathway Enrichment: Gene Ontology (GO), KEGG, Reactome, and Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA).
  • ✔ Biomarker Discovery & Network Biology: Protein-protein interaction (PPI) network construction and hub gene identification.
Multi-Omics Reporting Package
Differential Expression Matrices Annotated log2-fold-change tables with Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted p-values (FDR) and statistical thresholds.
Clustered Heatmaps & Volcano Plots Vector-quality publication charts illustrating sample clustering, PCA dispersion, and target gene expression profiles.
Interactive Pathway Diagrams Visual mapping of upregulated and downregulated cascades across canonical KEGG and Reactome networks.
